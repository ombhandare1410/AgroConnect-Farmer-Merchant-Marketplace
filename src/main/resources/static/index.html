How It Works

A clear path to doing business directly with one another.

  1. 01

    List produce

    Farmers share available produce and quantities.

  2. 02

    Discover & publish

    Merchants find produce or publish what they need.

  3. 03

    Connect

    Both parties express interest and arrange a conversation.

  4. 04

    Agree on a deal

    Discuss terms, propose a deal, and confirm it together.

  5. 05

    Keep clear records

    Record supply and payment details as both sides work together.

Supply and payment entries are records maintained by the participants; AgroConnect does not process payments.

About AgroConnect

Good produce deserves a clear path to market.

Farmers can struggle to find suitable buyers and obtain fair market value. Merchants need an easier way to discover available produce and connect with suppliers.

AgroConnect aims to bridge that gap through a centralized farmer–merchant marketplace, helping both sides discover opportunities and build transparent business relationships.
